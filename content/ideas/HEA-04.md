view: "Touch is a form of communication, and a vital one: it releases oxytocin,
  the hormone that makes us feel trust and love. It is healthy emotional food.
  Yet our social rules make it far easier to hug a pet or a young child than
  another adult. Love is not only physical touch, it speaks all five love
  languages, from words of affirmation to quality time. But tender daily
  touches, hugs and holding hands are part of it, and a city that makes room for
  them will need far fewer hospital beds."
