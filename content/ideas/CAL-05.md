view: >-
  Some of my most moving experiences have been with street musicians: an
  accordion player in a passage in Munich, whose music echoed beautifully
  between the buildings, and a guitarist in a street in Lisbon. Their music came
  from the heart, and it touched my heart. Birdsong is wonderful too, something
  I especially noticed when I lived in Australia. And children playing or adults
  talking can be lovely to hear, as long as it happens in the right place, not
  too close to someone who is trying to concentrate.


  A pleasant place is not only about sound: scents matter too, from rain and
  flowers to the eucalyptus of Australia’s gum trees.


  As Jean-Michel Basquiat is said to have put it: art is how we decorate space,
  and music is how we decorate time.
