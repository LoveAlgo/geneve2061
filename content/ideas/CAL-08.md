view: |-
  Most of the time, politicians fight against each other instead of caring for the people who elected them. This whole nonsense will be gone in 2061: you need two wings to fly, not just a right wing or a left wing.

  In a group, energy is generated when everyone points in the same direction, like the magnetic domains in physics; when they do not, energy is low and conflicts emerge. This is why consent works so well in small groups, like the partners of the Geneva bank Pictet, and why a big city should be organised as smaller communities where everyone’s voice counts. One of the aims of geneve2061.ch is to help with more structured, fact-based discussions, instead of confusing, unstructured and belligerent political debates.

  Journalism is a big part of the problem. The newspaper boxes in Geneva’s streets almost always put negativity forward, with carefully chosen negative words, supposedly to sell more newspapers. That should be forbidden. And the election posters that appear before every vote pollute public space even more than regular advertising.
