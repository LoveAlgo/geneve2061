  Older people in Geneva are among the most radiant people in town. They walk, cycle, dance and garden;
  they teach, mentor and volunteer; they live among younger neighbours rather than apart from them. Most
  stay healthy and independent until the very end of a long life, and growing old is something to look

  At 65, Swiss women can expect another 22 years of life, but only about 15 in good health
today_detail: >-
  Life expectancy at 65 is 22.4 years for women and 19.8 years for men in Switzerland, but healthy life
  expectancy at 65 is only 14.9 and 14.4 years. In Geneva, life expectancy at 65 is even higher: 22.8
  years for women and 20.5 for men.
today_source: >-

  2022 (2025)
today_facts:
- >-
  There is good news: since 1992, healthy life expectancy at 65 has risen by about three years in Switzerland,
  so the extra years can be healthy ones (same report).
- >-
  41.4% of Geneva residents aged 65 and over live with a long-term health problem, fewer than the Swiss
  average of 52.4% (same report).
- >-
  Difficulties with everyday tasks such as shopping, cooking or using public transport affect 21.2% of
  Geneva residents aged 65 to 79, and 56.9% from age 80 (same report).
- >-
  37.3% of Geneva residents aged 65 and over feel lonely at least sometimes (same report).
precedents:
- place: Taketoyo (Japan)
  text: >-
    opened community “salons” for older residents in 2007: places within walking distance, run by volunteers,
    for crafts, games, light exercise and activities with preschool children, for about one dollar a visit.
    Over five years, regular participants were about half as likely to lose their independence as non-participants
    (7.7% against 14.0%).
  source_needed: false
- place: Finland
  text: >-
    tested a two-year programme for 1,260 people aged 60 to 77 at risk of dementia, combining healthy
    eating, exercise, brain training and monitoring of heart-health risks. The participants’ overall cognitive
    scores were 25% higher than those of the control group, and their processing speed 150% higher.
  source_needed: false
