today_headline: >-
  The City of Geneva rejected an advertising ban by 51.9% in 2023, while neighbouring Vernier has had
  one since 2023
today_detail: >-
  On 12 March 2023, voters in the City of Geneva rejected the “Genève zéro pub” initiative by 51.9%. The
  city has about 4,713 billboards, of which 1,480 are reserved for commercial advertising. In November
  2024, the City awarded a new advertising concession for 2025 to 2029. Meanwhile Vernier, Geneva’s second-largest
  town, banned all commercial advertising visible from public space, and in June 2024 the Federal Supreme
  Court confirmed that the ban respects fundamental rights.

view: |-
  In most cases, advertising is pollution: visual pollution and often noise pollution. It is a by-product of a badly conceived machine, like the exhaust gases of petrol cars.

  There is a difference between informing people and forcefully pushing a message to persuade them to buy a low-quality product or something they don’t need. If you offer a quality product or service that people need, word of mouth will take care of the rest. A good website that search engines can find, a press release and a place on online maps cost little or nothing.

  With advertising, you spend money to pollute people’s lives. From my perspective, that’s a lose-lose situation.
