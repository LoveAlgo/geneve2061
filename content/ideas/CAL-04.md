view: >-
  Have you ever experienced true silence? It is rare for most of us, but it can
  be found in deserts and snowy mountains when there is no wind, in caves
  without running water, in deep basements with thick walls, and in recording
  studios. I once spent some time in a recording studio where I measured the
  sound level at 27 decibels. It felt so good, and it made me aware of the
  constant noise we live with in cities. Such places are healing, to a certain
  extent, and I sometimes wonder whether part of many musicians’ creativity
  comes from the calm they find in their studios.


  Libraries are my favourite silent places, to read and to work. Unfortunately,
  many public libraries also welcome loud children who play and sing, which is
  not compatible with people who want to concentrate, and only very few have
  separate rooms for quiet reading.
