view: |-
  Beauty comes from within as well as from outside: we cannot have perfect outer beauty with inner ugliness or negativity. In theory it is quite easy: make sure a person has their needs met, and we will automatically have a beautiful person. Physical exercise, love and lifelong learning are among the most important.

  In fashion, a few rules help. First, no advertising: unless you get paid to wear a logo, why wear one? Second, adapt your clothing to your local culture and climate. Third, find your own unique style: you are a unique work of art, so frame it in a unique way.

  Fashion is both a science and an art. Ideally, ask a stylist to help you choose the right colours and fit for your hair and skin colour, height and body type. Some people are colour blind or have little sense of aesthetics; asking a friend or a professional for help is nothing to be ashamed of.
