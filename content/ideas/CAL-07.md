view: >-
  Less information noise requires two things. The first is individual
  discipline: to stop mindless scrolling and ask ourselves what information we
  truly need in our lives. Many people who are very careful about what they put
  on their plates feed their brains with junk: gossip, negative news, violent
  films.


  The second problem is platforms that reward noise. Many YouTubers post again
  and again, rehashing the same information until they have hundreds of videos
  with mostly the same content. Platforms should select for quality instead of
  quantity: a creator with 30 to 50 quality videos should rank higher than one
  with 500. Creators themselves should remove older content that is no longer
  relevant, especially in fields like self-development, where more recent
  knowledge tends to be more helpful.


  Streaming platforms, too, seem geared more towards quantity than quality, and
  even libraries are not always good at curating quality books. This is a fairly
  recent phenomenon, and much more research and rethinking is needed.


  Do you feel more intelligent (educated), inspired or uplifted after having
  consumed a certain piece of information? If not, avoid similar content in the
  future.
