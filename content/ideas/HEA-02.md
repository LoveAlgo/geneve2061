today_facts:
- >-
  In Switzerland, suicide has been the leading cause of death among people under 50 since 1990 (Swiss
  Medical Weekly, 2024).
- >-
  In the canton of Geneva, 1,286 people took their own lives between 1995 and 2012, around 71 a year.
  More recent cantonal figures still need to be obtained from the cantonal statistics office (OCSTAT).
- >-
  Between 2021 and 2022, one in seven young people in Geneva had suicidal thoughts (study in Swiss Medical
  Weekly, 2024).
- >-
  Among Swiss people aged 65 to 84, around 80% of suicides in 2023 were assisted; above 85, more than
  90% (RTS analysis, 2025).

questions:
- >-
  What makes people choose assisted suicide, and what would make them want to stay? A CHUV study published
  in October 2026 interviewed 30 people engaged in the process and identified six dimensions behind the
  decision; it is a good starting point.
- >-
  How can loneliness be measured in Geneva, neighbourhood by neighbourhood, so help goes where it is needed?
- >-
  Which mix of prevention, purpose and community brings a whole city close to zero? No city or country
  has done it yet.
view: >-
  The number of suicides is far too high for such a wealthy country, and the rise in “exits” among older
  people is alarming. The aim is not to ban anything: assisted suicide is a legal choice in Switzerland.
  The aim is a society in which people are no longer pushed to check out early, because their lives are
  full of meaning and other people.
