view: >-
  Acoustics in buildings is largely a question of common sense, in the layout of
  the rooms, and of respect: not talking when others are trying to concentrate.
  At universities, most students are respectful, but there are too often some
  who talk and disturb everyone else.


  Another big problem in offices, libraries and other places where people need
  to concentrate is roadworks in the street or building repairs next door. I do
  not have a perfect solution for this yet, but the silent tools described in
  [[CAL-03]] are a good start. And so are soundproof building materials.


  In Geneva 2061, churches, mosques, synagogues and other places of worship are
  open day and night for anyone who wants to come for calm and concentration.
  Today, most of them are closed at night, if not all the time.
