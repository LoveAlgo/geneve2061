- >-
  Cycling is riskier in Geneva than elsewhere in Switzerland: 409 cycling accidents and 4 cyclists killed
  in 2025, a 10% rise in one year (Pro Vélo, cited in the same report).
- >-
  Serious injuries rose by 4.2% in 2024, and accidents involving people over 65 on e-bikes have almost
  tripled.
- >-
  In 2026, a resolution asking the canton to aim for “zero deaths and serious injuries” was rejected in
  committee by 9 votes to 6, without hearing any experts. Some members argued that figures per kilometre
  travelled would look better for Geneva, because of its many commuters.
