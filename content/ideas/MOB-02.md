view: |-
  If we have hardly any private cars in Geneva, it will not only remove traffic noise and the danger of accidents, but also the visual pollution of cars parked everywhere in the streets. That leaves room for trees, cycle lanes, café terraces in summer, or playgrounds. The shared cars parked on the outskirts can work like today’s car sharing, the red Mobility cars.

  Not owning a car has many other advantages: less stress in traffic jams, no money to earn for the car’s costs, no fines, no ugly garage disfiguring the house, and less focus on material things. By organising neighbourhoods wisely, a city could have twenty to thirty times fewer vehicles on its roads, without inventing any new technology.
