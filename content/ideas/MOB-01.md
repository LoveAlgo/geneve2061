view: >-
  Did you know that in Swedish, cycling (cykla) and happiness (lycka) are
  perfect anagrams? Yet bicycles and cars are no good fit on the same road, and
  very fast electric bicycles are no good fit with pedestrians. In Geneva 2061,
  dedicated lanes and streets make collisions very rare, and cyclists show more
  respect than many do today.


  More and more people will choose to live closer to where they work, so
  commutes will be much shorter on average. Nobody commutes more than 20 to 30
  minutes: someone living near a metro station on the outskirts reaches the city
  centre in the same time as someone living closer does by bicycle, or someone
  living in the centre does on foot.


  The idea is not new: the 15-minute city, where everything we need is within
  walking or cycling distance, is now discussed in many cities worldwide. But
  for it to create social harmony, each neighbourhood should also be a real
  community.
