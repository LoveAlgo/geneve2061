vision: >-
  Children in Geneva learn how their bodies work, how to cook fresh food, how to move, breathe, rest and
  handle stress, just as they learn to read and count. Adults keep learning in neighbourhood health centres
  built around a swimming pool, a kitchen and a garden. Everyone knows the difference between a symptom
  and its underlying cause, and takes responsibility for their own health, with doctors as advisers rather
  than the only authority.

today_detail: >-
  According to the Federal Office of Public Health, nearly half of the population reports frequent difficulties

view: >-
  Health is about much more than nutrition and exercise: back in 2007, I built a holistic health model
  with twelve components, from work and relationships to finances and mindset. The most important lesson
  is the difference between a symptom and its underlying cause: pills mostly treat symptoms, while addressing
  the cause stops the problem from coming back. Instead of leaving every decision to the people in white
  coats, we should educate ourselves and take responsibility for our own health, and find our own health
  tricks, because what works for one person may not work for another.
