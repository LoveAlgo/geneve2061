today_detail: The University of Geneva taught architecture from 1946, first in a
  school and from 1994 in an institute with the status of a faculty. Its closure
  was announced in March 2007, and the institute closed for good soon after.
  Architecture is still taught in Geneva at HEPIA, the cantonal university of
  applied sciences, while many Genevan architects study in Lausanne. In the
  canton, only professionals registered by the State as qualified (MPQ) may
  practise as architects.

  - In the United States, the University of Notre Dame’s School of Architecture
    teaches classical architecture and traditional urbanism, following
    Vitruvius, Palladio and Vignola. Its Driehaus Prize, created in 2003, was
    conceived as an alternative to the mainly modernist Pritzker Prize.
  - In France, the compagnonnage trains young people in the crafts of stone,
    wood, metal and other materials over about five years, as they travel from
    town to town. To be admitted, an apprentice must produce a masterwork.
    UNESCO listed it as intangible cultural heritage in 2010; it involves almost
    45,000 people.

view: >-
  Most people have been taught to accept ugly modern architecture as good
  architecture. Yet to get a house built, at least a dozen professionals are
  involved: the structural engineer, the electrician, the plumber, the council,
  the bricklayers, the carpenters, the interior and landscape designers. When an
  ugly, soulless building goes up, at least a dozen people have failed to
  question the architect.


  The more difficult part is to re-educate architects and re-organise the whole
  building industry. Designing and building a house is not easy, but it can be
  made easier with the right teachers, the right knowledge and the right design
  software.


  Lay people also need to take more interest in architecture, so that they can
  challenge the professionals. If an architect cannot explain why a building is
  designed a certain way, then some knowledge is missing. Keep asking why until
  you get a satisfactory answer: you will have to live with the consequences.
