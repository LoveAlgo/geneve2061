questions:
  - How much of an infrastructure budget should go to art and ornament, and does
    it pay for itself through use, care and pride?
  - Is it better to hide technical equipment such as electric boxes underground,
    or to make it beautiful where it stands?
  - Who chooses the artists, so that the result pleases the people who use it
    every day?
view: >-
  Everything we build should be a work of art, and good art is both functional
  and aesthetic. A retaining wall in a garden can be both. So can a metro
  station, a street lamp or an electric box.


  Ugliness can be turned into beauty. In Alice Springs, big satellite dishes
  were painted with Aboriginal art, and in northern France an old Second World
  War bunker was covered with mirrors. Many concrete walls in cities are now
  painted very creatively.


  Moscow and Paris show how loved a beautiful metro can be. Geneva’s new Léman
  Express stations are bright, but they lack the ornament and craftsmanship that
  would make people proud of them. The next stations can do much better.
