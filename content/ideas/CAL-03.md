today_facts:
  - Since March 2021, the European energy label for fridges and freezers shows
    the appliance’s noise in decibels and a noise class from A, the quietest, to
    D. Class C, for example, covers 36 to 41 dB(A) (Swedish National Agency for
    Public Procurement).
  - Switzerland sets noise emission limits for certain machines and requires
    their maximum noise level to be marked on them, under the ordinance on
    machine noise (Federal Office for the Environment).

  - Why are quiet professional tools not used everywhere, and what would make

  - How can ventilation and heat pumps, which energy-efficient buildings need,

view: >-
  Being hypersensitive to noise, I have been disturbed by machines in many
  places where I have lived. The fridge is a regular culprit: most fridges make
  so much noise that you cannot sleep in a room with one, or next to a kitchen
  with a noisy one. Ventilation and air conditioning are another, and the noise
  gets much worse when these devices do not work properly. Then there are
  blenders, vacuum cleaners and electric drills.


  A Hilti employee once told me that the company makes special, quieter tools
  for places such as hospitals. Why not use them everywhere? Even for industrial
  and construction machines, where noise cannot always be avoided, I am
  convinced that improvements are possible almost everywhere.
