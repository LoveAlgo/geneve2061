view: >-
  Many people try to make deliveries and logistics more efficient, but very few
  discuss what should be delivered in the first place. Just because we can
  transport almost anything across the oceans, doesn’t mean we should. Before
  ordering anything online from overseas, we should always ask whether there is
  a local alternative: I once met a pub owner in Adelaide who sources over 80%
  of what she needs locally. Local goals like Albi’s 60 km food radius at least
  make sure that food is not transported unnecessarily over long distances.


  In the future, drones may deliver small, urgent items, like the apéro wine on
  a sunny Sunday afternoon when you sit in the park or at the lakeshore. But
  they need to be silent, and there cannot be too many of them.
