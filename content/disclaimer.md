## About this site

Geneva 2061 is an independent, personal project by Henrik Hillerström. It is not affiliated with the Republic and Canton of Geneva, the City of Geneva, Grand Genève, the HUG or any other organisation mentioned on the site. Places and institutions are named only to describe a possible future for the city.

## A vision, not a forecast

The scenes of Geneva in 2061 come from a novel, *Grooters Book 1*. The ideas on this site are proposals for discussion, not plans or promises. Figures, studies and examples are given in good faith and were checked when they were written, but they may be incomplete or out of date. Please check the original sources before relying on them.

## Not professional advice

Some pages discuss health, the economy or the law. They offer general information only, and never replace advice from a doctor, a lawyer or another professional. If you are going through a hard time, you can talk to someone now: in Switzerland, call La Main Tendue on 143 (day and night) or, for young people, Pro Juventute on 147. In an emergency, call 144.

## Your privacy

This site has no accounts, forms or comments, and it never asks for your name or any other personal information.

- **Visit statistics:** the site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous statistics about visits, such as which pages are viewed, roughly which country or city visitors come from, the type of device, and how they found the site. Google Analytics uses cookies for this, and does not store visitors’ IP addresses. The data is processed by Google, possibly in the United States, under the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Frameworks. You can refuse these statistics by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing Google’s opt-out add-on ([tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)).
- **Fonts:** the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when a page opens.
- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security purposes.
